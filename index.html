    Private by design

    Everything is generated on your device with the browser's cryptographic random number generator. There are no network requests, analytics, cookies or third-party scripts.

    Password or passphrase?

    Random passwords give the most strength per character and suit a password manager. Passphrases are easier to type and remember; five or more words is strong.

    How strength is measured

    Strength is based on how the value was generated, not how it looks. Crack times assume an offline attacker trying 10 billion guesses per second.
